# Chinese phrase explanations

Literal meanings, intended meanings and player feelings for five Chinese expressions.

1. **策划是懂玩家的，懂得怎么悄悄改概率**

   **Literal:** “The designers understand players: they know how to change probabilities quietly.”
   **Intended:** Sarcastic accusation that designers changed gacha odds without disclosure.
   **Feeling and uncertainty:** Distrust and resentment. This is an allegation, not proof that odds changed.

2. **这波属于是逼氪了，懂的都懂**

   **Literal:** “This move amounts to forcing spending; those who know, know.”
   **Intended:** The player feels pressured to spend real money. 氪 means paid spending in gaming slang.
   **Feeling and uncertainty:** Frustration or cynical resignation. The specific purchase, mechanism and amount remain unspecified.

3. **艾琳=铁锅，新人别懵**

   **Literal:** “Ailin equals iron pot; newcomers, do not be confused.”
   **Intended:** “Iron pot” is a nickname for 艾琳. Ailin is a transliteration, not a verified official English name.
   **Feeling and uncertainty:** Informal explanation, possibly playful. No clear praise or criticism; the nickname's origin and character mechanics are unstated.

4. **白嫖党表示毫无压力 [doge]**

   **Literal:** “Free-to-play players report absolutely no pressure [doge].”
   **Intended:** Claims an untroubled experience without spending. Doge may add playfulness or irony.
   **Feeling and uncertainty:** Possibly relaxed, boastful or teasing. Sentiment remains uncertain without context; doge alone does not establish sarcasm.

5. **非酋报道，160抽一个金都没有 绷不住了**

   **Literal:** “An unlucky player reporting in: 160 pulls without one gold; I cannot take it.”
   **Intended:** Complains about a reported dry streak. “Gold” usually means a high-rarity result here.
   **Feeling and uncertainty:** Frustration and exasperation. The count is a self-report, not proof of changed rates or a broken guarantee. Banner and pity rules remain unknown.
